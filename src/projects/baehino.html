Context

Baehino is a Twitch personality and VTuber who describes herself as "a demon overlord from the Demon Association." Her style mixes cute and evil, so we designed a stream package around that mix and built it to hold up over long streams.

What We Did

We designed a full set of broadcast visuals and brand assets for Baehino's streams. Every element reflects her persona as a demon overlord.

Looping scenes built for 4+ hour streams, with a spooky scene transition and hand-drawn animated elements

Custom typography and a full rebrand that mixes cute and evil, set up so new scenes are quick to make

New profile panels, stream banners and social media assets that match across platforms

Design Process

Baehino's mix of cute and evil shaped every design choice, starting with a color palette that's inviting and a little sinister. We chose fonts that combine sharp, angular lines with rounded edges to match her playful "demon overlord" persona.

We carried the same style into her Twitch profile and social media, with new illustrations and icons for her profile panels. We hand-made her stream banners and social graphics so her channel and social accounts look like one brand.
