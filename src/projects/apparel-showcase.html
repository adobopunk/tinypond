Visually Striking Wearables.

We design apparel that people want to wear every day. Here are a few of our favorite projects.

Zero Enemies

A merch line built around a single phrase. We kept the design simple so the name stands out, with a look that's elegant and hard-edged and suits anyone.

Wine About It

A podcast hosted by Twitch creators Maya and QTCinderella, this project called for a band-style tee with a personal touch. Inspired by their chosen animals, we created a design that blends gritty, stylish aesthetics with a distinct personality.

QTCinderella – Staff Jackets

For QTCinderella's event staff, we designed a set of custom jackets. Each one has one of 30+ staff names built into the design.

Deco Deco

Merchandise for Deco Deco, an LA-based store. As part of our larger branding project with them, we designed custom shirts that match their visual identity and look good on their own.
