{{ title }}

UltraCaius hired Tiny Pond to build a new suite of visuals to coincide with the evolution of his VTuber model and a new era of streaming for the Kitsune of Eternal Blossom.

The project included social graphics, a cinematic animation and a new set of stream animations built for his new model.

What We Did

We built a complete stream graphics package around UltraCaius's character and the themes he asked for: celestial objects, torii gates, cherry blossoms and fantasy.

A 3D animation for his intermission screen

A full set of custom animated stream overlays

Social banners, schedule assets and custom branding

Celestial Scene

UltraCaius loves space, so we built his intermission scene as a night sky influenced by Makoto Shinkai's films. We made it in Blender with custom shaders and geometry nodes: a cherry blossom scene with auroras, a comet and rippling grass, all in UltraCaius's colors.
