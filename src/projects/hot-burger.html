Context

Hot Burger was a live event hosted by QTCinderella in Los Angeles, inspired by the chaotic fun of the Overcooked video game. Fans placed orders as if they were in the game, while QTCinderella and her friends cooked and fulfilled them in real time.

Tiny Pond built an event identity with the same fast, playful energy as Overcooked, for both the people at the venue and the viewers online.

What We Did

We handled creative direction from start to finish and designed every asset for the event.

A bold logo inspired by Overcooked, with tilted lettering, bright colors and a kitchen theme

Custom broadcast scenes, including split-screen layouts, break screens and lower thirds

Event signage, order packaging and branded set dressing for the venue

Design Process

We built the look around the chaotic fun of Overcooked, with a wood texture tying everything together and giving it a cozy kitchen feel.

The branding works across the broadcast graphics, signage and merchandise, so the event looked the same in person and on stream.

Brand Extensions

We extended the brand into merchandise and packaging that attendees could take home:

Collectible stickers with the Hot Burger logo

Custom aprons for QTCinderella and her friends, which became part of the event's look

Branded packaging for food orders and merchandise
