Context

Deco Deco is a Los Angeles-based arts & crafts studio specializing in Decoden, a Japanese craft of decorating everyday accessories so each one is personal.

We built their visual identity from scratch. It needed to show Deco Deco's playful, welcoming side and appeal to a creative audience that loves cute things.

What We Did

We developed a complete brand identity for Deco Deco, with every element designed around the studio's happy, cute style.

A custom logo in a soft script font that looks handmade

A warm pastel palette of light pink, peach and cream

Hand-illustrated icons (hearts, flowers and ribbons) and retro-inspired patterns

Design Process

We started by learning what Deco Deco is about. The brand needed to feel playful, feminine and creative.

We explored mood boards, color palettes, and typography options to establish a direction that felt both whimsical and grounded. We helped Deco Deco apply the identity across their web design, storefront, and social media posts.

The final identity mixes retro and modern styles, in the same spirit as Deco Deco's own creations.

Brand Extensions

We delivered full brand guidelines and a design that scales to any platform, so Deco Deco's team can extend the brand to new websites, merchandise and content on their own.

We designed custom T-shirts with the Deco Deco logo.

The identity carries over to Deco Deco's Shopify store.

A consistent look makes Deco Deco's posts easy to recognize on social media.
