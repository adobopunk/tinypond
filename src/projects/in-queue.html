Context

Corporate interests and profit-driven models run most of the esports tournament scene, and the competition itself often comes second.

Esports veterans founded In Queue to change that. It's an independent, worker-owned production and operations agency that runs community-driven competitions.

In Queue needed a brand identity that would stand out in a crowded field.

Design Process

Most esports branding is aggressive and over the top. We wanted something clean and confident that would look right on an indie LAN stream or a championship trophy. We explored a wide range of directions before choosing a logo that was distinctive and easy to animate.

The final mark has four outward-facing arrows, one for each of In Queue's core values: Integrity, Community, Opportunity and Excellence. Its emblem shape fits in with esports branding, and it moves like a game's matchmaking queue animation.

Putting Values Front and Center

In Queue's promise is to make competitive gaming more fun and more accessible. We launched the brand with a video that builds the logo one element at a time while stating what In Queue believes in and why.
