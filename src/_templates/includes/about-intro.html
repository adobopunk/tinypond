What is Tiny Pond?

Tiny Pond is a creative studio and consultancy started by Tifa Jade and Mateo Juliá Wise. We worked side by side at an agency for over three years, designing for gaming brands like Mountain Dew, Fortnite and Xbox.

Now we work with clients directly as Tiny Pond.

When you hire Tiny Pond, you work directly with the two artists making your design system and assets, and your budget goes to independent artists.

Tiny Pond is a new studio, but we've each spent years doing this work for major brands. If you have a project in mind, tell us about it.
