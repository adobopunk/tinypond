Tifa Jade Animation Director
she/her

tj@tinypond.studio

Tifa Jade, who also goes by TJ, is a creative producer and animation director with over 10 years of experience leading visual projects from concept to delivery. She spent more than four years in the UFC's post-production department, and her work has aired on UFC broadcasts, Fight Pass and ESPN.

Now a senior creative producer and freelance animation director, she leads animation for esports and gaming brands and has worked with Epic Games, Xbox and Mountain Dew on experiential marketing campaigns. She designs motion identities and animation for esports broadcasts, event promos and websites.

Mateo Juliá Wise Graphic Designer

mateo@tinypond.studio

Mateo is a graphic designer who works across many styles. As creative director of the esports company BoomTV, he designed experiential brand work for marketing campaigns and esports broadcasts with major gaming brands.

Mateo started designing while making event graphics for his college in NYC. He has since worked with brands, influencers and independent clients, and he likes to push visual identities toward experimental, bold options. He adapts quickly to each project and works fast.
