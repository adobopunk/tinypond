What clients say

About Tiny Pond

Tiny Pond is a creative studio and consultancy started by Tifa Jade and Mateo Juliá Wise. We worked side by side at an agency for over three years, designing for gaming brands like Mountain Dew, Fortnite and Xbox. Now we work with clients directly as Tiny Pond.

When you hire Tiny Pond, you work directly with the two artists making your design system and assets, and your budget goes to independent artists.

  • Tifa Jade she/her

    Animation Director

    TJ is a creative producer and animation director with over 10 years of experience leading visual projects from concept to delivery. She spent more than four years in the UFC's post-production department, and has led animation for Epic Games, Xbox and Mountain Dew campaigns.

  • Mateo Juliá Wise he/him

    Graphic Designer

How we work

  • Embrace collaboration. We get the best results by communicating openly and solving problems together with you.
  • Champion inclusivity. We partner with and support causes that make a positive difference, and everyone on our team is respected and heard.
  • Own your work. We take creative risks, and we can always explain the thinking behind them.
  • People first. Our work is made by people. We don't use generative AI to make it.
  • Trust in the pond. We keep you informed throughout a project, and we ask you to trust us with the creative decisions you hired us to make.
  • Never stop evolving. We keep learning new tools and techniques between projects.
  • Long-term partnerships. We'd rather work with you for years than once, and we plan each project with the next one in mind.
  • Do good, be good. We choose sustainable practices where we can and support projects with a positive social impact.
  • Excellence in everything. We aim to deliver more than you asked for and adapt to what each project needs.
